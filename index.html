About

Who we are

TopTronix 24/7 LLC is a Bakersfield-based field technical services company, in operation since 2012. The company services California DMV self-service kiosks throughout Central California and brings years of hands-on field experience with computers, cash registers and point-of-sale systems, appliances, assembly, and general repair.

Beyond technical work, TopTronix also handles occasional process serving and auditing of local stores. The company is also building its software development skills, with the goal of pairing hardware field service with software.
